## Purpose

Outside software I work in audiovisual production: cinematography, multi-camera direction, editing and color grading. This website presents that work under my own name. It is a portfolio of audiovisual work, not a software project: what belongs in this portfolio is the website itself.

## The experience

- **Image first**: a dark, cinematic identity where photography from real productions carries the page.
- **Selected work with roles**: each production states what it was and what my part in it was, from conference coverage to an eight-camera live album recording.
- **Capabilities**: production and post-production, described from planning and capture to the final image.
- **Behind the frame**: a process view of each production: context, cameras and decisions.

## What it represents

A personal brand website in a different discipline, built with the same approach I apply elsewhere: clear structure, honest attribution of roles and a design that suits the subject.
