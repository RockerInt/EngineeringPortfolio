## Purpose

Legado's is an editorial service that preserves personal and family stories as biographical books. Its tagline is *“Toda historia merece permanecer”* (every story deserves to remain). The website has to do what the service does: slow down, listen and make a story feel worth keeping.

## The experience

The site reads like the opening pages of a book rather than a sales page.

- **Editorial storytelling**: it opens with the brand alone on the page, then a first-person family memory, before any explanation of the service.
- **Brand presentation**: a serif, paper-toned identity that borrows from book design: generous margins, italic display type and small typographic details such as a work number.
- **The process, told as a craft**: three stages (listening, writing, legacy) explain how a book is made, from recorded conversations to the finished edition.
- **A single call to action**: one quiet invitation to start writing, repeated without pressure.

## What it represents

A brand-led website where typography and pacing carry the message. The technical work is in service of restraint: little interface, careful type and a narrative structure.
