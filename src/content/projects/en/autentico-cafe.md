## Purpose

Auténtico Café presents Colombian specialty coffee grown in the mountains of Huila. Its line is *“Autenticidad en cada sorbo”* — authenticity in every sip. The page has to explain why origin matters and lead a visitor from that story to the product.

## The experience

The page turns geography into navigation. It begins at the summit, around 2,000 metres above sea level, and scrolling becomes a descent through the altitudes where the coffee grows.

- **Origin** — the region, the growers and the conditions that shape the coffee.
- **Altitude as a through-line** — a persistent altitude marker ties each section to a point on the mountain.
- **Traceability** — a direct comparison between industrial coffee and coffee of origin.
- **Process** — from cultivation and harvest to processing and roasting, told as stages of the journey.
- **Varieties, products and ordering** — the story resolves into the coffees themselves and a way to buy.

## What it represents

A product landing page built around a single narrative idea, where layout, photography and scroll position all reinforce the same message about origin.
